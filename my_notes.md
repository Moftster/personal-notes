## Stock-Selection Checklist

- [ ] **Am I happy to hold it for at least a year?**
- [ ] **Is the company already profitable?**
- [ ] **Trailing Price-to-Earnings (P/E) > 10?**
- [ ] **Trailing Price-to-Earnings (P/E) ≤ 20?**
- [ ] **Forward Price-to-Earnings (P/E) (Uber purchase...) > 10?**
- [ ] **Forward Price-to-Earnings (P/E) (Uber purchase...) ≤ 20?**
- [ ] **What is the price-to-book ratio?**
- [ ] **How does the price-to-book ratio compare to other companies in the industry?**
- [ ] **Would I personally pay for—or already use—the product/service?**
- [ ] **Does the CEO (and wider leadership) seem impressive and trustworthy?**
- [ ] **Is the brand “cool” or at least resonant with its target customers?**
- [ ] **Am I comfortable making this 1 of the 2 new stocks I allow myself to buy this year?**
- [ ] **Is revenue/earnings growth on the right trajectory (avoid another “Intel” situation)?**
- [ ] **Do I genuinely understand how the business makes money?**
- [ ] **How much debt does the company carry? Look at the Debt-to-Equity ratio or Total Debt vs. Cash. High debt can be a significant risk.**
- [ ] **Does the company generate strong Free Cash Flow (FCF)? Profit is good, but cash flow pays the bills and funds growth/dividends.**
- [ ] **How does the company's P/E compare to others in the industry?**

# Reflection on Megan & James's wedding, 15 April 2025
The reason I am writing this is that I froze when I delivered my reading at Megan and James's wedding. Well, I didn't exactly freeze, but I lost my breath and struggled to catch it. The words felt stilted and I struggled to get them out. Despite Hannah swearing on Annis and Mae's lives that she couldn't tell, I could and I feel like everyone else in the room could. My lingering thought is that I sullied their wedding. That I got it off on a bad footing, that they were embarrased and that people were unconfortable becasuse of me.

Anyway, I want to take lessons away from this. As I have noted to myself many times before, public speaking is a lottery. Sometimes it goes well and sometimes it doesn't. The key thing I need to remember is the other side of the maxim: that *despite all the practice and preparation in the world*, sometimes it just doesn't go well.

That is what was missing this time around. I didn't practice enough. I didn't prepare enough. I didn't do everything in my power to give myself the best chance of performing well in the situation. I took it for granted that I'd be able to read a short poem off a piece of paper. Recently I've been presenting regularly at work (admittedly on Teams) and it has been going well, so I got complacent. I thought I could just rock up and do it. I didn't practice enough, I read the poem out loud around 5 or so times. That is not me and the results showed.

I need to learn from this and on reflection these are the key points I need to remember:

## Practice, practice, practice
You can't practice enough. Read it out loud until you can do it without looking at the page. Then do it some more. If people will listen, practice in front of them. Practice in front of the mirror. Practice in front of a camera. Practice holding your body in the right way and using your hands. Practice intonation and your pauses. Practice, practice, practice. Then practice some more.

## Visualise
Picture the room, picture the people, picture the pressure. See yourself there in front of the room, dressed as you will be on the day. See yourself standing up and walking to the front of the room. See yourself standing there looking at the people in the room, smiling and breathing calmly. See yourself starting to speak clearly and confidently. See yourself abosultely smashing it and people coming up to you afterwards to say how well you did.

## Breathe
Breathe properly beforehand. As the moment approaches and the nerves start to build, breathe in through your nose and out through your nose. Make sure the out breath is at least the same duration as the in breath (possibly try box breathing) and feel yourself calming down as you breathe slowly.

## Speak to God
I speak to God every day. I ask Him to guide me and give me the strength and the courage to do what I need to do. I ask Him to help me be the best version of myself: father, husband, developer, son, brother, friend. I should ask Him to help me, guide me and support me when I'm public speaking. Ultimately it is in His hands and part of His plan.

In summary, I should never underestimate anything (in this instance public speaking, but ultimately anything I care about and want to go well). Public speaking for me has always been a challenge and I know in this instance I took it too lightly. I'd love to get back on the horse and do it again, have a chance for redemption, but that is not going to happen. I need to learn from this and remember that public speaking for me is always somewhat of a lottery but in this case I didn't do everything possible within my power to give myself the best chance of success.

# 🎤 Public Speaking Checklist

A guide for doing everything in my power to give myself the best chance of success.

## ✅ Preparation :triumph:

- [ ] **Read the material aloud** at least 50 times in advance.
- [ ] **Practice until it's near-memorised**, then keep practicing.
- [ ] **Record myself** and watch it back.
- [ ] **Practice in front of others** (partner, friends, kids).
- [ ] **Work on body language** – shoulders back, head up, grounded stance.
- [ ] **Use hands naturally** – don't fidget or hide them.
- [ ] **Practice intonation and pauses** – no rushing, let the words land.


## 🧠 Visualisation
- [ ] **Picture the room** – layout, lighting, people.
- [ ] **Picture myself walking up**, calmly and confidently.
- [ ] **Visualise success** – clear speech, connection with the audience, positive feedback.
- [ ] **Dress rehearsal** – wear what I’ll wear on the day during at least one practice.

## 🫁 Breathing

- [ ] **Breathe in through nose, out through nose** – keep it slow and steady.
- [ ] **Practice box breathing** (4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold) before speaking.
- [ ] **Slow breath before speaking** – anchor myself in the moment.

## 🙏 Grounding in Faith

- [ ] **Speak to God** - ask for his strength and support.
- [ ] **Remind myself**: this is part of His plan.
- [ ] **Offer the moment** – for His glory, not my ego.

## 🦶 Physical Prep

- [ ] **Do 45 minutes of cardio** earlier in the day – I always find myself more eloquent, fluent, and fluid after a workout.
- [ ] **Stay hydrated** – Lubriacate those vocal chords and the distraction of needing a wee isn't such a bad thing.

## 🚫 Pitfalls to Avoid

- [ ] **Don’t underestimate the moment** – even short readings deserve full attention.
- [ ] **Don’t rely on past success** – every moment is its own challenge.
- [ ] **Don’t leave practice to the last minute** – preparation is love and respect.

## 💬 Final Affirmation

> "I’ve done everything I can. Now I trust God and I trust myself."
